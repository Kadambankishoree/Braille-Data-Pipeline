Key Elements of Knowledge Management

* Knowledge Management is an integral part of Business Intelligence, as it enables
organizations to turn raw data into meaningful insights and supports effective decision-
making at all levels. A well-implemented KM strategy enhances the value of BI by
ensuring that knowledge is effectively captured, shared, and utilized across the
organization.
